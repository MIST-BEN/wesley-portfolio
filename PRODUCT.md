## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: HR and interviewers screening for frontend internships and campus roles. They arrive from a résumé link or a direct message, often on a phone or a laptop between other candidates, and spend seconds deciding whether to read further. Their job: confirm that the candidate has built real, working software, what it was built with, and how to reach him.

Secondary (not designed for first): frontend team leads who open case studies and GitHub to judge engineering thinking.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual (Chinese default, English toggle) personal portfolio for Wan Wenxuan, a frontend engineer in Chongqing. Success means a recruiter can, within one screen, see real project work, open a live demo, and find a way to make contact.

## Positioning

A student of Materials Forming & Control Engineering (2023–2027) who builds frontend through self-directed study and project work. The angle stated across earlier editions: shaping material and shaping interfaces are the same craft. Projects are real and public where possible; a working live demo exists for the main project.

## Operating Context

- Visitors open the site from résumé PDFs, chat apps (WeChat/QQ) and email; many on mobile.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages at wesley.loan (CNAME in this repo). Must load acceptably from mainland China: third-party CDNs (jsDelivr) are best-effort with system fallbacks.
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build tool. Pages are generated by `../scripts/build_v8.py` (outside this repo); edit copy there.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: home (`index.html`), two case studies (`case-shopping.html`, `case-devtools.html`).
- Bilingual via `data-zh` / `data-en` attributes and `app.js`; language persists in localStorage.
- Quick-overview dialog per project, copy-email button, full-screen menu.
- Motion is minimal and must respect a visitor toggle (off) and the OS reduced-motion setting.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before prose: real screenshots and a live demo outrank descriptions.
2. A recruiter should understand who, what, and how to contact within one screen, on a phone.
3. Every claim traces to a repo, a demo, or the user's own words; illustrations are labelled.
4. Chinese first, English complete; neither language is an afterthought.
5. Effects serve the work; they never hide or delay it.
